# Optional audio and video production

Start from the current reviewed evidence and text. Enable this route only when the run requests media. The repository contains production instructions; it does not include a speech service, service credentials or a background rendering system.

## Choose a generation route

Record the required provider, language, speaker arrangement, target duration and available access. Confirm the product's current supported settings before generation. If Google audio is required, use the agreed Google product and preserve its generation record; do not silently substitute another voice service.

Two common routes require different checks:

- A source-based conversation, such as a NotebookLM Audio Overview, can reorganize or add wording. Preserve the source packet and generation prompt, then check what the resulting audio actually says.
- Script-led speech generation, such as an applicable Gemini speech workflow, starts from supplied lines. Check omissions, additions, pronunciation and speaker assignment against the recording script; do not assume every line was spoken correctly.

Consult the current official documentation for [NotebookLM Audio Overviews](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16212820) and [Gemini speech generation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/speech-generation) before choosing product settings. Access, models and available controls may differ by account or product version.

Use only material authorized for the selected service. Keep account identifiers, private source links and episode files in the private run record. A workflow template does not authorize uploading material to another service.

## Prepare the audio brief

Include the episode question, selected evidence, listening order, must-retain figures and qualifications, difficult pronunciations and common misreadings. Keep the recording script separate from the media-generation prompt. If two speakers are requested, give each useful explanatory work; repeated agreement and staged surprise should not carry the conversation.

If introductions, music or closing segments are required, obtain the permitted originals and record their versions. Set the body duration in relation to the total target. Do not add branded assets merely because a previous episode used them.

## Preserve and inspect the result

1. Save the original download with its actual extension. Record product, generation date, selected input versions, visible settings and source duration. A filename ending in `.mp3` does not prove its encoding or provider.
2. Check the generated content against the evidence, especially names, numbers, dates, negations, attribution and conditions. Verify any material assertion introduced by the audio; remove or regenerate unsupported content.
3. Edit from the clearest available original. Document cuts, inserted segments and gain changes. Avoid repeated lossy exports; inspect the final encoding and measured duration.
4. Listen to the complete exported audio through an actually available audio review capability or an identified listener. Check intelligibility, speaker levels, pacing, joins, clipping and missing speech. If complete listening did not occur, record that limitation explicitly; waveform statistics and transcript checks do not establish perceived clarity.
5. Transcribe the final audio, including inserted segments. Correct speaker labels and important terms against that exact file. The final transcript is a record of the audio, not a copy of the earlier script. After an audio edit, update and recheck the transcript.

A generation success is not an audio approval. If original download, content verification or complete listening is blocked, identify that stage and the artifacts actually available. Do not label an unverified file as a finished Google MP3.

## Add video when requested

Lock the audio before assigning final storyboard times. Each shot should record its start/end, corresponding spoken point, screen text, asset filename and any source/date/unit the viewer needs. Choose the aspect ratio from the requested channel. Use permitted visual assets, and put exact data or terminology in editable text layers when image generation cannot reproduce it reliably.

Review the exported video for legibility at the intended viewing size, timing, spelling, data labels, audio sync and transitions. Inspect the actual export rather than relying on the editing timeline alone. Subsequent cuts require corresponding transcript, subtitle and storyboard updates.

## Media handoff record

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Requested and actual provider | Product and provenance evidence |
| Inputs | Exact script/source versions and any permitted bookends |
| Outputs | Original download, edited audio, actual transcript; video assets if requested |
| Duration | Measured total and whether bookends count toward the target |
| Review | Who or which agent performed each check, when, and on which file hash |
| Remaining gaps | Missing access, unverified content, incomplete listening or export problems |

Store private generation links and source files with the run, not in this public repository. Deliver finished files to the authorized destination; keep source, script and transcript labels distinct.
