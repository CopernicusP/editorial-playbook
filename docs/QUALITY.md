# Review quality and delivery readiness

Review should locate and repair failures a reader or listener would encounter. Fluency, an AI-detector score and checklist completion do not establish accuracy or usefulness.

Use the pinned core's [review and repair workflow](https://github.com/CopernicusP/evidence-to-article/blob/81ff2d1a15cbc9e2cf2cfb1c3baf2c14a5747153/skills/evidence-to-article/SKILL.md). The playbook adds package-level acceptance around that work.

## Review responsibilities

Give each applicable criterion to a different independent reviewer. A lane groups related work in the core manifest. Two criteria in the same lane still need different reviewers. Supply each specialist with the inputs needed for their test. The cold reader first sees only the article and a description of the intended audience. Give the other specialists the relevant original brief and evidence. Withhold prior verdicts. A specialist may review the same criterion in both languages. Keep the criterion tied to each article and preserve each reviewer's independence across the whole assignment.

| Criterion | Question for its reviewer |
| --- | --- |
| Prose and readability | Where would the intended reader lose the thread? Quote the passage, explain the difficulty and identify a concrete repair. |
| Source fidelity | Does the actual source support each material claim, including its units, period, denominator, attribution and uncertainty? |
| Numerical evidence choice | Which figures establish scale, proportions, timing, obligations or a consequential comparison? Flag distracting figures and any cut that would leave the reader unable to judge magnitude or tradeoffs. Explain what inference each retained figure supports. |
| Reader implications | What changes for the intended reader? When several parties are involved, trace what each contributes, gains, gives up or remains exposed to. Attribute a stated purpose to its source and label plausible incentives as inference. |
| Reader teach-back | With only the article and the audience description, explain who does what, how one step leads to the next and why the result matters. If a step depends on unexplained knowledge, quote the first such passage. Record the actual retelling before consulting the source packet. |
| Closing synthesis | State the question raised by the opening, then quote the ending's answer. Look for a supported conclusion, any condition that limits it and any gap between question and answer. |
| Revision continuity | Compare the agreed brief and prior fixes to the exact current text. Where a comparison was commissioned, identify both source endpoints, what changed or held, and why the difference matters. Quote each required passage and record the source of any superseding decision. |
| Comment closure | Trace each accessible comment ID and original anchor to its revised passage. Record what was fixed, deliberately retained or remains open, and why. |
| Delivery | Compare the actual exports with the reviewed text. Inspect the requested format, render, length and media where applicable. |

For example, a synthetic notice says a town pool will open a week later than scheduled and gives its total visitor count last summer. The source reviewer can verify both figures. The numerical-evidence reviewer decides which helps a family arranging lessons. The reader-implications reviewer checks whether a postponement of lessons follows from the notice. Record an agent's reading as an editorial judgment. Claim direct reader feedback only when someone in that audience actually gave it. For a new unaided test after a revision, use a reader who has not seen the answer key. If an actual reader still cannot explain a passage, ask one targeted question such as “What did you think would happen after this step?” Use their answer to locate the missing explanation. If no answer is available, keep the uncertainty visible and continue evidence-supported repairs.

## Integrate and review the delivered version

One writer reconciles findings against the evidence and revises the complete article. Use the [feedback record](../templates/editor-feedback.md) to connect a finding or comment to the original passage, its disposition, the revised passage and its recheck. Keep unavailable comments or unresolved objections visible.

Affected specialists re-read the revised passage in context, including downstream effects. The other specialists inspect the changes before renewing their own decisions. After all specialists finish, give every current article and the brief to one fresh reviewer who did not write, integrate or perform a specialist review anywhere in the run. Bind that reading to all the canonical hashes. Ask for a whole-article verdict. Record each reviewer's actual identity, type and criterion, exact artifact hashes, timing, findings and limitations. With limited concurrency, queue distinct reviewers. With no available independent review, report the specific gaps and deliver a limited-review draft.

If the writer edits the article after these reviews, renew the affected specialist checks and the fresh whole-article read on the changed version. Rebuild and recheck its exports. Check any subsequently produced documents or media on their actual final files. Record precisely which forms each reviewer inspected. Retain superseded decisions in the private history.

After two unsuccessful repairs of the same issue, change the approach or disclose the precise remaining issue. Do not repeat cosmetic rewrites and describe each one as a successful improvement.

## Check what the audience receives

- Count using the selected convention. Inspect actual document pages where the target is expressed in pages.
- Compare deliverables with their audited canonical articles. Check encoding, headings, visible text, links and source notes.
- Preserve meaningful uncertainty, attribution and date distinctions while improving readability.
- Complete the [media checks](MEDIA.md) on the actual exported audio and video. Record full listening honestly.
- Synchronize transcripts, storyboards and subtitles after changing audio or the final timeline.
- Treat local rendering and destination-platform previews as distinct checks.

Targets marked advisory produce a warning outside the suggested range. Strict requirements belong in a custom preset with required enforcement. Neither repeated prose nor fabricated measurements are acceptable ways to meet a target.

## Understand the two checks

The core checker verifies article records, exact claim quotations, declared source access, current review hashes, counts and canonical TXT/HTML consistency. It does not itself open evidence or judge the prose.

The playbook's delivery checker verifies the selected package, file integrity, required output records, canonical associations, review attestations and related audio versions. It reruns the locked core checker instead of trusting a saved success report. It cannot prove that a named reviewer actually read a source or listened to an audio file.

Keep these limits visible in the [acceptance report](DELIVERY-CONTRACT.md). `semantic_verification_performed: false` describes the script, even when real independent reviewers have performed semantic review and recorded it.

## Learn from edits

Capture what an editor changed and why. Adopt a recurring preference as a scoped rule with a language, channel, audience and example. Keep one-off factual corrections in the run. Do not blend different authors' preferences into a shared voice.

Evaluate changes on comparable tasks using actual factual repairs, structural rewrites, editing effort and reader feedback. A favorable response to one draft is useful evidence, not proof of broad effectiveness. Public examples should be synthetic or explicitly cleared.

## Hand over accurately

Complete routine editorial decisions within the task. Escalate a consequential unresolved purpose, indispensable evidence/access or genuinely missing required authorization rather than returning a generic request for someone to “humanize” every paragraph.

A ready package satisfies the file and recorded-check contract. Human or organization approval, when actually required, has its own state and current package binding. Sending and publication remain separate authorized actions.
