## Research instructions

Find original evidence for the claims likely to determine the article's conclusion. Record source identity, publication date, event/data period and precise locators. Distinguish a source's assertion from an independently established fact. Reconcile material contradictions; show calculations and units for derived quantities. Retain counterevidence that changes the answer.

Return a packet containing the answer supported so far, relevant background and chronology, key findings, source-linked claims, calculations, important limits and unresolved questions. Identify essential unavailable evidence. Do not use unsupported anecdotes or imagined reactions to supply a narrative.

If research already exists, read it first and investigate material gaps or time-sensitive status. Record inherited verification separately from sources actually opened in this run. Use a product name such as Deep Research only when that product was actually used.
